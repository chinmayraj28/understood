## Problem Statement

Build a **working prototype of a voice-driven product** that shows how a voice AI agent meaningfully improves a user's experience. It should do one of these:

- remove friction,
- simplify a process, or
- make something possible that wasn't possible before.

The challenge is about where voice AI goes **next**, not where it is today: how people will connect, create and communicate tomorrow.

You must say clearly **why voice is the right interface** for the key moments. Valid reasons include:

- hands are busy
- eyes are busy
- accessibility
- real-time interaction
- emotion carried in the voice

> If a keyboard would do the job just as well, pick a different moment.

The brief is left vague on purpose. Hiya judges the **choice of problem and approach** as much as the code.

## Examples

**Example 1: good fit**
```
Scenario: A mechanic under a car asks for the torque spec and next repair step.
Why voice: Hands are busy and dirty, eyes are on the engine. A keyboard is not an option.
Verdict: ✅ Voice is the right interface.
```

**Example 2: good fit**
```
Scenario: A caller's voice is checked live for signs of a cloned or synthetic voice.
Why voice: The signal itself is in the audio. No other input carries it.
Verdict: ✅ Voice is the right interface (Security track).
```

**Example 3: poor fit**
```
Scenario: A user speaks a search query to filter a spreadsheet while sitting at a desk.
Why voice: None. Typing is just as fast and more precise.
Verdict: ❌ Pick a different moment.
```
