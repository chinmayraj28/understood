# Ordinary speech for counting false wakes: none of these should wake it. Long lines, so each
# one is a stretch of background talk. Several mention "hands free" and start with "hey".
- Hey, did you see the match last night? It went to penalties in the end and I could barely watch. My brother kept shouting at the television, and the neighbours probably thought something terrible had happened. Anyway, are we still meeting for lunch on Thursday, or should we move it to Friday?
- The new car has hands free calling built in, which is great because I spend so much time driving to work. You just press the button on the wheel and say who you want to call. The only problem is that it sometimes calls the wrong person, and last week it rang my old landlord by mistake.
- Hey there, can you grab the milk on your way home? We are also out of bread and those biscuits the kids like. Oh, and if the pharmacy is still open, could you pick up my prescription? They said it would be ready after three o'clock today.
- I read an article about hands-free kitchen taps. They turn on when you wave your hand under them, so you never touch the handle with dirty hands. It seems like a small thing, but apparently it saves a lot of water, and it is easier for people with arthritis.
- Hey, how are you doing? I have not seen you in ages. Did you move house in the end? Last time we spoke you were looking at a flat near the station, the one with the small garden and the very steep stairs.
- Okay, so the plan for the weekend is this. On Saturday morning we drive to the coast and have breakfast at the cafe by the harbour. In the afternoon we walk along the cliffs if the weather is good, and on Sunday we come back slowly and stop at the market on the way.
- My grandmother uses a hands free headset for her phone now, because holding it up to her ear made her arm ache. She says the voice on the other end sounds clearer too. She still forgets to charge it, though, so half the time it is flat when somebody rings.
- Hi, sorry I am late. The bus broke down halfway here and we all had to wait for the next one in the rain. Hey, is that coffee for me? Thank you, you are a lifesaver. Right, shall we get started? I think the first item on the agenda is the budget for next year.
